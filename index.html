FAQ

What is Remote Online Notarization (RON)?
Remote Online Notarization allows documents to be notarized over a secure video call using an approved digital platform. Virginia is a RON-enabled state. You'll receive a link prior to your appointment and sign using an electronic signature.
What documents do I need to bring to my appointment?
For in-person signings, bring a valid, government-issued photo ID (driver's license, passport, or state ID). For loan signings, the title company typically provides the document package. For RON sessions, your ID is verified digitally during the video call.
Do you serve all of Virginia?
Yes. Mobile notary services are available throughout Northern Virginia, Richmond, Hampton Roads, and surrounding areas. Remote Online Notarization is available statewide — and to clients in other states — as permitted by Virginia law.
What are your hours and do you offer weekend appointments?
We offer flexible scheduling including evenings and Saturdays to accommodate busy professionals and families. Use the booking calendar to see available slots, or contact us directly to discuss your timing needs.
How much does notarization cost?
Fees vary by service type and travel distance for mobile appointments. Please see the Pricing & Fees section for a full breakdown, or contact us directly for a custom quote.
Can you notarize documents for someone in a hospital or care facility?
Yes. We regularly provide mobile notary services to hospitals, assisted living facilities, and rehabilitation centers. Please call ahead so we can coordinate with the facility and confirm the signer's availability.

Important Appointment Requirements & Legal Notice

Thank you for booking your appointment! To ensure a smooth and successful signing, please review the critical legal and operational policies below:

  • Bring Valid, Unexpired Government ID: All signers must present an unexpired, government-issued photo ID (such as a Driver's License or Passport). Digital IDs or photos of IDs cannot be accepted.
  • Complete Your Documents Ahead of Time: Please ensure all forms are completely filled out except for the signatures and initials, which must be executed in front of the notary.
  • Legal Notice (UPL): By law, a Virginia Notary Public is an impartial witness and not an attorney. I am strictly prohibited from giving legal advice, interpreting your documents, or helping you choose or fill out legal forms. If you have questions about your paperwork, please consult a licensed attorney prior to our meeting.
  • Late & Wait Policies: Out of respect for all clients, appointments operate on a strict schedule. A $15 late fee applies after 10 minutes. For mobile or specialty facility visits (including jails), a wait fee of $15 per 30 minutes will be assessed for any delays.
  • Sunday Bookings: Please note that appointments scheduled on Sundays are subject to a $50 specialty service fee.
  • Cancellation/Forfeiture Policy: If your appointment cannot be completed because a signer lacks proper identification, lacks the mental capacity or willingness to sign, or paperwork is missing, your upfront booking deposit will be forfeited.

Notary Is Not an Attorney

Finding Your Place Notary, LLC and its commissioned notary provide notarization services only. The notary is not an attorney, cannot provide legal advice, cannot assist with document selection, and cannot help you fill out or interpret legal forms. For legal questions, please consult a licensed attorney.
