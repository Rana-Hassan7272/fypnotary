FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions

What is Remote Online Notarization (RON)?
Remote Online Notarization allows documents to be notarized over a secure video call using an approved digital platform. Virginia is a RON-enabled state, and all sessions are fully compliant with state law. You'll receive a link prior to your appointment and sign using an electronic signature.
What documents do I need to bring to my appointment?
For in-person signings, please bring a valid, government-issued photo ID (driver's license, passport, or state ID). For loan signings, the title company will typically provide the full document package beforehand. For RON sessions, your ID will be verified digitally during the video call.
Do you serve all of Virginia?
Yes. Mobile notary services are available throughout Northern Virginia, Richmond, Hampton Roads, and surrounding areas. Remote Online Notarization is available statewide — and to clients in other states — as permitted by Virginia law.
What are your hours and do you offer weekend appointments?
We offer flexible scheduling including evenings and weekends to accommodate busy professionals and families. Use the booking calendar above to see available slots, or contact us directly to discuss your timing needs.
How much does notarization cost?
Fees vary by service type and travel distance for mobile appointments. Virginia sets a maximum fee per notarial act. Please contact us directly or book a consultation for a specific quote tailored to your document needs.
Can you notarize documents for someone who is in a hospital or care facility?
Yes. We regularly provide mobile notary services to hospitals, assisted living facilities, and rehabilitation centers. Please call ahead so we can coordinate with the facility and confirm the signer's availability and capacity.
